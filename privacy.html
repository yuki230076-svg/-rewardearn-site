RewardEarn may work with third-party advertising, offerwall, survey, and reward providers. These providers may process device or transaction information as necessary to provide their services, attribute completed offers, prevent fraud, and provide rewards.

Use of Information

Information may be used to provide and improve RewardEarn, attribute completed activities, calculate rewards, prevent fraud, maintain account security, and provide customer support.

Data Retention

Information will be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law.

RewardEarn may use third-party service providers for advertising, offer delivery, analytics, hosting, authentication, and rewards. Their handling of information is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Children

RewardEarn is not intended for children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as RewardEarn develops and new features or services are introduced.

Contact
